How it works

From report to removal

  1. 01

    Send Us Your Information

    Call Apex or send us your report, photos and project information.

  2. 02

    We Review the Scope

    We review the identified materials, quantities, access, containment requirements, disposal requirements and project conditions.

    If additional information or testing is required before we can properly quote the work, we'll tell you.

  3. 03

    You Receive Your Quote

    We provide a clear scope and pricing based on the information available.

    No mystery scope. No unnecessary complications.

  4. 04

    Documentation & Project Setup

    Once the work proceeds, Apex prepares the required project documentation, risk assessments and regulatory filings applicable to the work.

  5. 05

    Safe Removal

    Our trained personnel complete the abatement using procedures appropriate to the material and risk level.

  6. 06

    Project Closeout

    Where applicable, we coordinate clearance requirements, complete our project documentation and turn the work area back over so the next stage of your project can proceed.

Send it to us.

Include the report, project address or city, photos if available and a short description of the work you're planning.

Can you remove asbestos from homes?

Yes. Apex performs residential asbestos abatement as well as commercial and industrial work.

Stop disturbing the affected material and provide us with any testing or hazardous materials documentation you have.
